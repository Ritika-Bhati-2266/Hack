# Previse Beta — Onboarding Note (First 50 Users)

Welcome to the Previse beta! Here's how to get started in 2 minutes:

## 1. Open the app
Go to the app URL shared with you. You'll land on the **Dashboard** with demo data.

## 2. Connect your real data (Connect tab)
- **Option A — Account Aggregator:** Click "Connect via AA", approve, then "Fetch My Data".
- **Option B — CSV upload:** Export your bank statement as CSV
  (columns: `date, narration, amount, type`) and upload it.
  Enter your **current bank balance** for accurate results.

## 3. Simulate a purchase (Simulator tab)
Type what you want to buy + amount, pick Cash / EMI / Loan, hit **Simulate**.
You'll get a verdict: **SAFE TO BUY / WAIT / EMI OK**, plus before/after
buffer, runway, and goal impact.

## 4. Track goals (Dashboard + Profile tabs)
Check your Financial Firewall, goals progress, and monthly commitments.

---

## ⚠️ Important — please read

- **Agar aapka data reset lagta hai, dobara Connect tab se reconnect karo.**
  Sessions expire after 1 hour for privacy — your bank data is never stored
  permanently. Reconnecting via AA or CSV restores everything in seconds.
- Your data stays in your browser session only. Use **Profile → Delete My Data**
  anytime to erase everything immediately.
- This is a beta: verdicts are rule-based estimates, not financial advice.
